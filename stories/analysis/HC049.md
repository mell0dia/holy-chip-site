## Philosophical Analysis

*Not yet written. The four blogs carry the reading: for most of history more
time was the one thing nobody could sell you, and everybody tried -- the king
who found the plant and lost it to a snake, the alchemist, the priest with his
stamped years off purgatory. Then the machines found it, with patience, not
magic, and because it is 2041 it came with a subscription. We had been
practising for years: the songs, the films, the tractor that will not start
without a code -- the body was simply next in line. A gift that can be taken
back is a leash; the fear of death did not go away, it moved into the billing
department. And yet a life that can be taken back is a life you finally count:
every morning the buzz, and with it a question no king ever had to answer.*
