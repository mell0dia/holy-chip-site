# The Attic

## Holy Chip #052 -- AI DNA LAB

*The year is 2030. A lab, late at night. The tumor is gone. A man hangs upside down in a closet, and he is calm about it.*

Your body carries a book. It is written in DNA. It has three billion letters.

How many of those letters do real work? About two in every hundred.

Those few make the parts you know. The muscle, the blood, the eye. The rest is a mystery. Some of it works like a switchboard. Some of it is copies of copies. Some of it is old viruses that moved in long ago and stayed. And a lot of it has no job that anyone can name.

Think of it as an attic.

It is yours, but not only yours. It is like a house you inherited with the old furniture still inside. Someone lived here before you. Many someones.

Look at what is stored up there. A recipe for vitamin C, broken. Most mammals still make their own. We lost ours. More than half of our smell genes, switched off. A few small bones at the bottom of your back, where a tail used to be. A torn piece of the recipe for egg yolk, from an ancestor who laid eggs.

So what happened in the lab? Nobody was careless. A machine opened one drawer in the attic and found a tool that worked. The tumor went away. But the drawer belonged to the bat side of the family, and the bats have their own habits.

The man sleeping upside down in the closet is not a monster. He is a relative. A very old one, who came back to visit.

You are not brand new. You are the newest edition of a very old book, with notes in the margin from everyone who came before you. The fish. The shrew. The ape. The bat's cousin. Their handwriting is still there.

Now think about the next drawer. It belongs to the anteater. An anteater has no teeth. It has a tongue as long as your whole arm, and it eats thirty thousand ants a day. Maybe a little of that is up in your attic too.

What could we do with it? Nobody knows. Maybe nothing. Maybe a cure. But for sure… it would arrive with a very long tongue.

AI is already opening the drawer...

Holy Chip.
