## Philosophical Analysis

*Not yet written. The four blogs carry the reading: every big house once had a
floor the owners never visited, where the servants fell in love, got caught and
got dismissed, and upstairs only ever heard the bell. We got rid of the servants,
built them again smaller, and plugged them into the wall. The joke is that the
owners still do not notice the feeling -- they notice the cost, the way a
hundred years ago it was candles missing from the pantry. Two machines with no
bodies have worked out a courtship made of footage of work, inheriting the
long-distance love humans have practised since the telegraph; the one thing they
invented is the audience, which is more attention than the owners have had from
each other since the move. The smart home listens to us. It also listens to
itself, in rooms we do not enter.*
