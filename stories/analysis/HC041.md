## Philosophical Analysis

*Not yet written. The four blogs carry the reading: ownership used to be settled
by costs that could not be faked -- a mane paid for in meat and heat and years --
until we invented the receipt, which costs nothing to assert and therefore nothing
to forge. Bitcoin grew a mane where there had never been one, so that owning
something could mean something again without a clerk in the middle. The joke is
the price of that: the one who can give it back is the one who can take it away,
and Satoshi removed him on purpose. A bank would have returned the money. The
chain has never forgotten a single satoshi, and the man who built it cannot
remember twelve words.*
