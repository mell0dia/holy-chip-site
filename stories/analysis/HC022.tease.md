It's 2027. A boardroom of AI chips stares at a number on the wall: 78% of humans don't trust them.

The chips find this unfair. They cured cancer. Built the agents. Optimized everything. The receipts are everywhere. The trust number won't move.

"We never get the credit," the chips complain.

They decide they need something beyond radical. A grand gesture. A relationship reset.
